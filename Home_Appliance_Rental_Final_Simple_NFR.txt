FINAL - Practical Non-Functional Requirements (NFR) and Core Business Rules | Version 3.0 | 13 October

Scope: A simple, reliable, attractive rental marketplace - not an enterprise-level platform. Responsive web
first; mobile-friendly design across phones, tablets, and laptops.
1. Project overview
One account can borrow appliances and list appliances for rent. Support multiple products, multiple lenders
in a single checkout, daily/weekly/monthly rental plans, security deposits, third-party delivery, return
inspections and dispute resolution.
2. Confirmed business rules
Rule
Final decision
Platform commission
Fixed 15% of product market value, per booked item/rental transaction.
Security deposit
Fixed 20% of product market value per item; refundable subject to inspection and
valid deductions.
Multiple lenders
A single cart/checkout may include products from different lenders; create separate
lender sub-orders.
Logistics
Delivery and pickup outsourced to a third-party delivery app/provider.
Duration
Daily, weekly and monthly rentals.
Damage and disputes
Lender inspects returned item and proposes evidenced deductions; admin reviews
disputed claims.

4. Rental process
• Lender adds appliance with category, condition, market value, rental rates, photos, location/serviceability,
deposit and availability.

• Borrower chooses items and rental dates; system checks availability for each item and reserves stock to

• Cart shows itemized rent, 15% market-value commission treatment, 20% deposits, logistics fees and final

• Checkout creates one parent order and lender-specific sub-orders. Payment success must be confirmed

• Undisputed eligible deposit is refunded; disputed amount is held for admin review while any undisputed
balance is released under policy.
5. Money logic - critical decision to implement safely
Example: market value Rs 20,000, rent Rs 2,000: commission = Rs 3,000 (15% of market value), deposit =
Rs 4,000 (20% of market value). The deposit is not platform revenue. Because commission exceeds rent,
the checkout must explicitly show who pays the commission and obtain consent; the app must not silently
deduct Rs 3,000 from a Rs 2,000 lender rental payout.
Before launch, approve whether the borrower pays commission as a separate fee, the lender pays it, or a
defined split applies. Also finalize tax treatment, gateway charges, payout schedule, inspection deadline,
refund timeline, cancellation fees and late-return policy. Do not promise instant refunds; actual settlement
depends on payment provider.
6. Deposit and damage safeguards
• Record deposit separately from rental revenue; maintain a ledger of collection, hold, authorized
deductions, refunds and payment references.
• Require timestamped pre-handover and post-return photos plus condition checklist; record accessories
and serial number where applicable.
• Normal wear and tear is not automatically chargeable; publish a damage schedule and fair-evidence
standard.
• Lender cannot unilaterally finalize disputed deductions. Admin reviews evidence, records reason and
decision, and informs both parties.
• Use a compliant payment partner and approved fund flow for deposit collection/holding/refunds; do not
assume the platform can operate its own escrow account.
7. Simple but polished UX/UI
• Modern, clean visual design with consistent spacing, strong contrast, readable typography, attractive
appliance cards and clear product photos.
• Mobile-first responsive layouts for Android/iPhone, tablets, MacBook and desktop; touch targets at least
44px.
• Home: search bar, categories, location/service area, featured items; detail: photos, availability calendar,
rental plans, deposit and total-cost breakdown.

8. Practical non-functional targets
Area
Target / acceptance
Speed
Typical pages interactive within 3 seconds on a normal mobile connection; API p95 under 800

Security
HTTPS, hashed passwords, server-side role checks, validated uploads, secure payment

No double booking; idempotent payment/refund processing; retry failed delivery updates
safely.
Data
Daily encrypted backups; tested restore before launch; retain records under documented
policy.
Privacy
Collect minimum personal data, role-restrict access, provide privacy policy and consent.
Compatibility
Latest two major Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge versions; responsive 360px to desktop widths.
Observability
Error logs, payment/refund alerts, admin issue dashboard and basic uptime monitoring.
9. Recommended affordable tech stack
Frontend: React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS. Backend: FastAPI or Node.js (choose one). Database:
PostgreSQL. Image storage: managed object storage. Payment: compliant Indian payment gateway.
Delivery: third-party provider API if available, with manual admin fallback. Hosting: a small managed
cloud/VPS initially; scale only when traffic requires it. Mobile apps can be added later if needed; a
responsive web/PWA is sufficient for the first release.
10. Minimum database entities
User, Address, ApplianceListing, AppliancePhoto, AvailabilityBlock, RentalRate, Cart, Order, OrderItem,
LenderSubOrder, Payment, DepositLedgerEntry, DeliveryJob, HandoverEvidence, ReturnInspection,
DamageClaim, Dispute, Refund, Payout, Notification, AuditEvent.

12. Out of scope for first release
Advanced AI recommendations, dynamic pricing, complex insurance integrations, sophisticated fraud
scoring, microservices, native iOS/Android apps and enterprise analytics are not necessary for launch.
Status: Core product scope and six user decisions confirmed. Fee payer, inspection time limits,
tax/settlement mechanics and delivery provider contract remain launch-blocking policy decisions.
